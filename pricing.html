Billing questions

Pricing & billing FAQ

How is my price actually worked out?
We start with roughly how many calls your business takes in a month and which features you need, then put together a quote to match. No generic tiers, so you're never paying for capacity you don't use.
What if my call volume changes?
We'll never cut your calls off. If your volume grows or drops for a while, get in touch and we'll adjust your plan to match, no surprise fees.
Is there a setup fee or contract?
Currently we have no setup fee, and no lock-in contract on any plan. Billed monthly you can cancel any time; pay annually instead and get 20% off.
Do you offer a free trial?
In your setup meeting we'll build a custom AI receptionist for your business so you can call in and talk to it yourself before it goes live. Beyond that, every plan is billed monthly, so you can walk away any time if it's not for you.
