Billing questions

Pricing & billing FAQ

Can I switch plans later?
Yes, you can upgrade or downgrade at any time from your account settings and it takes effect straight away. You'll only ever pay for the plan you're on for the time you're on it, so you won't be charged twice for the same period.
What happens if I go over my call limit?
We'll never cut your calls off. If you consistently go over your plan's limit, we'll simply suggest the next plan up. No surprise fees.
Is there a setup fee or contract?
Currently we have no setup fee, and no lock-in contract on any plan. Monthly plans can be cancelled any time; annual plans are billed upfront at a 20% discount.
Do you offer a free trial?
In your setup meeting we'll build a custom AI receptionist for your business so you can call in and talk to it yourself before it goes live. Beyond that, every plan is billed monthly, so you can walk away any time if it's not for you.
