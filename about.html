About Callsmith

Built by people who know what a missed call costs

Callsmith started with a simple frustration: great tradespeople losing good jobs, not because of their work, but because nobody picked up the phone.

Our story

Callsmith was founded after watching a mate, a solid electrician with more work than he could handle, lose a $4,000 job because he was up a roof cavity when the call came in, and the customer just rang the next name on the list.

That's not a talent problem or a pricing problem. It's a phone problem. Tradies are brilliant at the work and stretched thin on everything else, especially answering calls the second they come in, every single time.

So we built Callsmith: an AI receptionist trained specifically on how trade businesses actually take bookings, qualify jobs, and handle urgency, so no call goes unanswered again.
