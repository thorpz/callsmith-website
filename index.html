FAQ

Questions tradies actually ask

Can't find your answer here? See the full FAQ or book a demo and we'll walk you through it live.

How long does setup take?
Most businesses are live within the week, and some are up and running in a much shorter time than that, depending on how much customisation you want.
Will it sound robotic to my customers?
No, Callsmith uses natural, conversational voice AI trained on real trade calls. Most callers don't realise they're not speaking to a person until you tell them.
Does it work with my calendar and CRM?
Yes, and it's not limited to a fixed list. Callsmith can connect to whichever calendar and trade CRM you already use.
What happens with emergency calls?
You set the rules. Urgent jobs, like a burst pipe or no power, can be flagged and routed straight to your mobile or on-call crew instantly.
Is my call data secure?
Every call is encrypted in transit and at rest. Transcripts and recordings are only accessible to you and your team, and can be deleted at any time.
Can I cancel any time?
Yes, all plans are month to month with no lock-in contract. Annual billing is available for a 20% discount if you'd rather pay upfront.
